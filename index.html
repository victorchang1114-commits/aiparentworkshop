一份啱用的練習。One small goal.
A better-fit activity.

把日常需要變成清楚的 AI 指令。先選目標，再複製例子，最後和孩子試一次。

Turn an everyday learning need into a clear AI prompt. Choose a goal, adapt an example and try it together.

T

Task · 任務

想 AI 做甚麼？
例如：五分鐘口語遊戲。

What should AI make?
For example, a five-minute speaking game.

C

Context · 情境

給誰用？已做到甚麼？
年級＋實際能力＋一個小目標。

Who is it for? What can they do?
Year level, current ability and one goal.

R

Reference · 參考／要求

用甚麼材料、格式及提示？
提供示範，另列答案。

Which material, format and support?
Include a model and separate answers.

我的第一個指令My first prompt

只在此頁整理內容，不會送往 AI。重新整理會清除填寫內容；需要保留可先複製或列印。

This page does not send your entries to AI. Entries clear on reload; copy or print to keep them.

選一個例子，改成你需要的版本。Choose an example. Make it yours.

英文版指令亦可要求產生中文教材。遊戲指令需配合支援互動輸出的工具；一般聊天工具可先製作紙本玩法。

English prompts can still request Chinese materials. Interactive games need a compatible tool; ordinary chat tools can draft paper activities instead.

用之前，停一停。 / Check before use.

AI 協助起草，家長負責核對。角色指令不代表專業審核；這是一般在家練習材料，不作診斷或個別治療建議。

AI drafts; parents review. A role prompt does not provide professional review. These are general home-learning materials, not diagnosis or individual treatment advice.

試一次，只改一樣。 / Try it. Change one thing.

可貼給 AI：「保留原本目標，請只修改＿＿，因為孩子＿＿。其他部分先不變。」

Try: “Keep the same goal. Change only ____ because my child ____. Leave the other parts unchanged.”
